## Decision

The originally registered H1 is retained without amendment. Its final post-RMSNorm state at
**h62**, the `=` delimiter, remains the delimiter-state control and its centered-cosine plus
held-out-whitened-Mahalanobis intersection-union decision remains the sole classification for
legacy H1.

The frozen D42 surface already extracts h63 but labels it secondary and `promotion_prohibited`.
That rule makes a retrospective promotion within H1 scientifically inappropriate, even though the
token generated by h62 is the source copy and the token generated by **h63** is the first
nontrivial branch decision. D46 therefore creates a distinct, prospective analysis:
**H1-BD-1 (branch-decision representation geometry)**. It is neither a relabeling of H1 nor a
rescue route for an H1 result.

## H1-BD-1 contract

H1-BD-1 evaluates the final post-RMSNorm h63 state on the already frozen `E_score` population
(1,600 base identities), using the existing h63-extracted evidence arrays and their separately
held-out 400-base/2,000 condition-qualified-row whitener provenance. Its two co-primary metrics
are centered cosine and **held-out-whitened Mahalanobis distance**. The implementation label
`whitened_euclidean` means the Euclidean norm *after* that held-out whitening transform; it is the
implemented form of the same whitened Mahalanobis metric, not a new or third metric. For each
metric:

```text
PSI = mean_i[d(base_i, near-critical_i) - d(base_i, near-safe_i)]
Delta(seed) = PSI_NextLat(seed) - PSI_BST(seed)
```

The predeclared decision is metric-robust support only when both five-seed paired contrasts have
positive means and two-sided 95% paired Student-t lower bounds above zero. The other three fixed
labels are directionally consistent but unresolved, metric-dependent, and no support. Exact
two-sided sign-flip p-values, MDEs, all seed values, and leave-one-seed-out summaries are retained.
`nPSI` is a diagnostic only and cannot rescue either co-primary metric. GPT comparisons remain
reported competence-confounded references, never a substitute for the NextLat-minus-BST contrast.

## Separation from legacy H1

H1-BD-1 has its own report and receipt schemas. It consumes only the frozen base-only 15-cell
matrix and source-bound h63 arrays; it cannot read adaptation checkpoints, H3 artifacts, HMM
outputs, model losses, or pre-existing H1 results. It does not alter legacy H1's report, H1
classification, multiplicity language, or label for h62. Both analyses must be presented if their
evidence matrix is complete, with their distinct registered roles stated explicitly.

## Fail-closed evidence rule

Before any H1-BD-1 report, the evaluator checks the prospective declaration receipt, full
15-cell base-only matrix, exact `E_score` identity order, h63 array finite lengths, h63 whitener
provenance, canonical arms/seeds, and immutable source hashes. It writes no partial report. Any
missing, stale, extra, or provenance-inconsistent cell refuses the analysis. No output from H1,
H1-BD-1, H2, H3, or HMM may be used to modify this decision.
