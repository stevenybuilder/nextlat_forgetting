# Scientific history

The legacy program was not throwaway work. It built the execution and measurement substrate and
found the optimization instability that now motivates the first study. It also accumulated a long
chain of study identifiers and operational decisions that made the active scientific question hard
to see. Those records were removed from the working tree but remain recoverable from public Git
history.

## What remains scientifically useful

- A pinned NextLat source tree and materialized GPT, NextLat, and BST configurations.
- Exact Path-Star generation, solving, competence evaluation, representation extraction, and
  checkpoint provenance.
- The seed-1235 topology, sampler, compiler, and first-gradient diagnostics.
- A balanced replacement generator for structural-overlap/future-conflict interventions and a
  fixed-site activation-patching implementation.
- HMM process, belief-update, probe, and evaluator scaffolding.
- Durable execution, recovery, hashing, and profile tooling.
- Tests for many scientific and provenance invariants.

## What is not carried forward as evidence

- The original five seeds are not enough for a reliable method-population claim.
- The retired representation pilot did not meet its frozen matching rule.
- The first controlled-forgetting stimulus bank confounded low-overlap cells and is not pooled with
  its repaired successor.
- The project-designed HMM family was withdrawn before confirmatory analysis; published Mess3 and
  RRXOR processes replace it.
- Provider incident documents and autonomous-agent process notes are operational history, not
  scientific authority.
- Deferred TinyStories/FineWeb plans are outside the current four-study program.

## History policy

The old Markdown decision chain, paper mirror, review notes, and former root documents are retained
in public commit `5c71e5f`. They are snapshots and may contain obsolete identifiers, statuses,
provider facts, and relative paths. Retrieve a specific file with `git show 5c71e5f:<path>` only
when it is needed to establish provenance for a legacy decision or artifact.

Existing source, scripts, configs, manifests, and compact results have not been bulk-moved because
their paths are embedded in hashes, tests, and recovery receipts. Their directory READMEs mark the
boundary, and new work will use descriptive `studies/` namespaces.

No historical outcome has been erased. Cleanup here means keeping active claims legible while Git
history remains the provenance ledger for failed and superseded experiments.
