# D43 — outcome-blind measurement amendment after training began

**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Lifecycle:** confirmatory training started; exactly 10 of 30 HMM cells reached update 3,000.  
**Outcome visibility:** no HMM or Lure-Star scientific evaluation was run, opened, or interpreted.

## Decision

D42 repaired an incomplete implementation of measurements already required by the frozen
scientific design. Because ten HMM cells were trained before that repair, the project may not
pretend this is a fresh pre-compute preregistration. It may continue only through the mechanical
D43 gate. The gate binds the original `a962…` archive, the independently cleared D41 operational
successor `ab4f4d…`, and one exact D43 successor archive. The `ab4f4d…` archive adds only the
two independently reviewed Colab pre-upload recovery-safety files to historical D41 successor
`9d1…`; its full `a962…` to `ab4f4d…` changed-path set is exact and receipt-bound.

The permitted delta is closed and file-specific: evaluation, aggregation, representation
extraction, atomic evaluation orchestration, semantic-evidence construction, their tests, and the
D42/D43 decision records. Each changed file is bound by its before and after SHA-256 in a frozen
declaration. Merely appearing on the allowlist is insufficient. Any other source change blocks.
Before any live semantic witness or test receipt is trusted, every packaged file must also match
the live tree byte-for-byte. Thus a rehashed archive with defective measurement code cannot borrow
evidence from a different working tree. The mixed HMM orchestrator receives a sanitized full-module
AST check: only the four all-30 evaluation preflight/evaluation nodes and the one exact
`COMPLETION_SUMMARY` import may change. Every other import, top-level statement or conditional,
model-set/update-count assignment, matrix constructor, launcher, training command, and training
phase must remain identical.

## Immutable scientific inputs and trained payloads

All training objectives, model and optimizer implementations, configs, update counts, frozen
data, manifests, split identities, and uploaded input generations remain unchanged. The original
input inventory must remain exactly `33fbf435…`. The exact D41 receipt must still identify the ten
ordered persistent-moderate jobs and their generation-pinned state, checkpoint, and sidecar
objects. Those payloads remain explicitly predecessor-created and successor-consumed read-only at
update 3,000; D43 neither retrains nor evaluates them while issuing this amendment.

## Evidence required before continuation

Issuance requires a successor-bound full-suite PASS, current Lure-Star v4/HMM aggregate v3
semantic witnesses, and an independent outcome-blind review stating `P0=0` and `P1=0`. A separate
two-party no-outcome attestation must state truthfully that training started, ten cells completed,
zero evaluator invocations occurred, and no scientific metric was inspected. The atomic
continuation state must partition the frozen 30-cell HMM matrix into the exact ten completed jobs
and exact twenty pending jobs, with no evaluated jobs.

Gate-10 semantic witnesses are consumed only from the unique source-scoped
`g10-lurestar_schema_receipt.json` execution-attestation payload with its exact role, schema,
artifact SHA-256, source archive, and canonical payload hash. Summary-check copies or an arbitrary
passing artifact cannot substitute for that executed witness set; its names and payload must equal
live source recomputation exactly.

The no-outcome attestation binds the independent reviewer's identity and exact review-report hash.
The review receipt reciprocally binds the attestation hash without a hash cycle. The report must
explicitly state all five lifecycle facts: training started, exactly ten HMM training cells
completed, scientific evaluations not started, zero evaluator invocations, and scientific metrics
not inspected. A reviewer name copied into an otherwise controller-authored file is insufficient.

After D43 passes, the next legal action is training the remaining twenty HMM cells. Scientific
evaluation remains forbidden until all 30 cells pass the separately implemented atomic preflight.
Any source, schema, evidence, checkpoint, input, lifecycle, or review change invalidates D43 and
requires a new outcome-blind review. This amendment never authorizes looking at preliminary
outcomes, selecting metrics or layers, changing hypotheses, or reviving retired Lure-Star H3.
