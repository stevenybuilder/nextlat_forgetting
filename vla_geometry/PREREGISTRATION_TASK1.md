# Superseded Task 1 preregistration

The original frozen design is preserved in `config/pilot.json`. It used 48 cells from the official
VIMA-Bench `visual_manipulation` combinatorial partition, eight representation seeds per cell, and
twenty separate behavior seeds per cell. Its primary test was the association between cross-fitted
factor interference and cell success.

The design was retired after the literature audit identified the official VIMA-92M Task 1 L2
result as 100% and the feasibility run reproduced that ceiling. See
`results/FEASIBILITY_TASK1.md`. No Task 1 outcome is used in the replacement study.
